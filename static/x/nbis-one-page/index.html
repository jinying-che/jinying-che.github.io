交付和收入向后移

11 月 12 日公布的当季营收含约 3.25 亿美元一次性确认。部分第三方机房延期后，全年营收落到原指引低端，原定 12 月达到的年化收入目标也推到次年 2 月。末批新泽西微软产能排到 2027 Q2。[3][19]

支出上调，先垫更多钱

2026 年资本开支指引升至约 270 亿美元，比原区间中值高 20%。2027 年计划支出中值又是营收中值的 3.4 倍。可收费产能延期会推后收入与部分回款，设备采购和建设却需提前支付，扩大了对外部资金的依赖。[3]融资压力

行业融资也在变贵

财报前 Reuters 已报道银行和债券投资人对 AI 债务更谨慎，并援引知情人士称 Nebius 搁置了高收益债计划。11 月下旬同行和科技股也受 AI 投资回报与举债担忧压制。行业下跌解释了共同方向，NBIS 在财报日比同行跌得更多，交付与支出变化是公司层面的额外压力。[20][21]

固定 9 月底净债务、经济股数和同一 2027 年营收假设（125 亿美元）回看，11 月 11 日收盘隐含约 4.43 倍 EV/营收，11 月 27 日约为 2.79 倍。市场对每一美元远期营收给出的估值大幅下降。[3][4][9]测算口径

产品与公司沿革

2024 年 7 月，荷兰注册的上市主体 Yandex N.V. 完成俄罗斯业务剥离，同年 10 月以 NBIS 恢复纳斯达克交易，并以 Nebius 发展国际 AI 云业务。到 2026 Q3，Nebius AI Cloud 已贡献集团约 99% 的营收。Avride（自动驾驶）和 TripleTen（职业教育）仍在集团内，ClickHouse 和 Toloka 是非上市股权敞口。[1][2][4][16]

AI Cloud 提供用于训练与推理的 GPU 集群、网络和存储，可用 Kubernetes 或 Slurm 管理任务。Token Factory 对外提供托管推理，客户也可以通过 Tavily 接入搜索。基础设施规模影响交付成本，软件层和工程支持影响客户迁移的成本。[1][3][13a][13b]

怎样把算力变成收入

专用集群
多年包量合同锁定合格产能的收入。交付速度与服务可用性决定客户何时开始付费。
公有 AI 云与推理
按用量计费或预留容量。用 GPU 集群利用率、实际成交价格和续约来判断单位产能回报。
轻资产合作
合作方出资建设部分产能，Nebius 在部署架构、启用平台及产能上线后确认相应收入，可降低自有资本投入，初期项目收入更容易波动。[1][3]

直接竞逐大型 GPU 集群。2026 Q3 营收为 36.61 亿美元，约为 Nebius 的 3.7 倍，规模可带来采购和部署经验。[2][10]

AWS Trainium 和 Google TPU 提供专用芯片选项，云厂商也能打包销售存储和网络。微软向 Nebius 租用算力，同时投资自建产能。[1][11][12]

以电力起家的 IREN

IREN 与微软签有约 97 亿美元 GPU 云合同，含 20% 预付。它也能将电力资源转成客户愿意提前锁定的 AI 产能。[14]

订单转成营收需要多久

截至 2026 年 9 月底，387 亿美元 RPO 中，预计约 40% 在未来两年确认，另约 40% 在随后两年确认。RPO 含递延收入及未开票合同金额，含可变对价估计，且排除原期限不超过一年的义务。合同上限因此无法直接加总为 RPO。[4]

前三大客户合计占 Q3 营收约 70%，其中最大客户贡献 33%。财报未披露各客户身份。公司还披露，从 Q3 初到股东信发布，客户新增承诺的合同额超过 120 亿美元，并与 Databricks、Datadog、Snowflake 等签下多年协议。[4][3]

最关键的交付指标

管理层预计 2026 年底签约电力达 5 GW，并计划每年新增上线超过 1 GW。签约电力要经过通电、集群安装与服务验收，才会变成可收费产能。新泽西微软项目因转向永久供电调整工期，预计年底交付过半，余下批次已排到 2027 Q2。2027 年起新增产能预计主要来自自建园区。[3]

管理层称可比的 1–3 年新合同，估算投资回收期由 Q2 的 22 个月缩至 Q3 的 19 个月。1–5 年合约的加权年合同额也从每兆瓦超过 2,000 万美元升至超过 2,500 万。回收期来自管理层估算，投产后的利用率与成本决定实际回报。[3]

融资风险如何传导给股东

抵押贷款与续贷
7 月约 7.73 亿美元贷款利率为 Term SOFR + 2.50%，借款实体需维持至少 1.15 倍偿债覆盖率，并已在 9 月末达标。10 月的 35 亿美元 Citi 担保桥贷只有 364 天，初始利率为 Term SOFR + 2.75%，利差可升至 3.50%，另有最低流动性约束。披露明确的是贷款额度。若提款，约一年内就要用现金偿还或转换为长期资金。[4][7a][7b]
可转债仍需偿还
8 月发行的 57.5 亿美元可转债，初始转股价为 $313.46 与 $324.65。若持有到期且未转股，约 66.7 亿美元本金需偿还，另有票息。股价长期低迷时，依靠转股化解债务的可能性更低。[4]
低股价放大增发稀释
Q3 公司通过按市价销售库存股筹得净额约 10.49 亿美元，均价 $229.14。若都按相同的 10 亿美元募资总额举例，按该均价需售约 436 万股，按 11 月 27 日收盘价 $89.46 需售约 1,118 万股，是原来的 2.6 倍。[4][9]

Q3 确认当期利息费用 1.13 亿美元，另有 0.92 亿美元借款利息计入在建资产成本。看项目回报时两者都要考虑。[4][15]

估值基准采用 2026 年 11 月 27 日收盘

当前价格隐含了什么

用 $89.46 收盘价乘以约 3.140 亿股经济等价股（9 月底 A/B 类流通在外股份加约 2,107 万份预付认股权证），股权市值约 280.9 亿美元。加账面债务 141.9 亿、减现金及现金等价物 74.3 亿，粗略企业价值约 348.5 亿美元，相当于 2027 营收指引的 2.68–2.90 倍。[2][3][4][9]

以 9 月底资产负债表估算。预付认股权证源于 NVIDIA 2026 年 3 月的 20 亿美元投资。可转债计入债务，未再加转换股数。未纳入经营租赁负债、未行权的其他奖励或单独扣除非上市股权价值。10 月后融资及现金变化将在后续披露中反映。

基准假设下，安全边际有限

如果 2027 营收达到指引中值 125 亿美元，年末净债务升至 120 亿美元、经济股数增至 3.35 亿股，维持 $89.46 需要约 3.36 倍 EV/营收。以 3.25 倍计算，每股约 $85.45。

基准情景中，倍数每减少 0.5 倍，每股价值下降约 $18.66；净债务每增加 50 亿美元，每股价值下降约 $14.93。若可转债被转为股份，应同时减少净债务并增加股数。

  3. 2027 年下半年盈利与资金回报除了 Q4 税前盈亏平衡，更关注折旧前后的利润、扣除客户预付时点影响的经营现金流，以及期末净债务与股数。[3][6]

哪些信号会削弱投资逻辑

工程与集中度
电力接入、液冷或 GPU 供应延误，可能引发大客户履约折让或递延确认。少数客户的验收节奏足以显著改变整个季度。
利用率与硬件寿命
新一代 GPU 或自研芯片压缩旧集群的租价与残值。续约率和可比新签合同的回收期若变差，盈利质量会随之下降。
融资与每股价值
预付款覆盖率下降、贷款利差扩大、可转债转股价或股票增发价偏低，都会削弱股东获得的增量回报。[1][3][4][7a]

研究结论　Nebius 已经证明大客户愿意提前承诺算力。如果延期产能如期补上、预付与低成本融资能持续，营收增长才更有机会转化为每股回报。按更新后的收盘价，基准情景的每股估值低约 4.5%。

The remaining New Jersey tranches for Microsoft are scheduled for Q2 2027. [3][19]

Spending rose and cash goes out first

The 2026 CapEx guide rose to about $27B, 20% above the earlier midpoint. The 2027 spending midpoint is 3.4 times the revenue midpoint. Before earnings, Reuters reported that banks and bond investors were more cautious about AI debt and, citing a source familiar with the matter, that Nebius had shelved plans for a high-yield issue. AI peers and tech stocks also came under pressure in late November over borrowing and investment returns. The sector move explains a common direction. The larger earnings-day decline points to additional pressure from delivery and spending at Nebius. [20][21]

From GPU clusters to managed inference

Amsterdam headquarters · Founder and CEO Arkady Volozh

What Nebius sells

The Dutch-incorporated public company formerly named Yandex N.V. completed the divestiture of its Russian businesses in July 2024, resumed Nasdaq trading as NBIS that October, and developed its international AI cloud business as Nebius. Nebius AI Cloud accounted for about 99% of group revenue in Q3 2026. The group includes autonomous vehicle developer Avride and tech education provider TripleTen, and holds private stakes in ClickHouse and Toloka. [1][2][4][16]

AI Cloud supplies GPU clusters, networking and storage for training and inference, with Kubernetes and Slurm options. Token Factory offers managed inference and Tavily adds search. Infrastructure scale shapes delivery costs, while the software layer and engineering support affect switching costs. [1][3][13a][13b]

How capacity becomes revenue

Dedicated clusters
Multiyear reservations lock in revenue for qualifying capacity. Delivery and service availability determine when customers start paying.
Public AI cloud and inference
Customers pay by usage or reserve capacity. Utilization, realized prices and renewals determine returns per unit of capacity.
Asset-light partnerships
Partners fund some of the capacity. Nebius earns revenue when it enables its architecture and platform and as capacity goes live. This can reduce capital requirements and make early revenue uneven. [1][3]

Who competes for the same budget

CoreWeave

Competes directly for large GPU contracts. Q3 2026 revenue was $3.661B, about 3.7× Nebius’s, giving CoreWeave more procurement and deployment scale. [2][10]

Hyperscalers

AWS Trainium and Google TPU offer custom chip options. Hyperscalers can bundle compute with storage and networking. Microsoft rents Nebius capacity while also investing in its own. [1][11][12]

Power-focused IREN

IREN signed a roughly $9.7B GPU cloud contract with Microsoft that included a 20% prepayment. It can also turn access to power into capacity that customers will reserve in advance. [14]

When contracted work becomes revenue

Of the $38.7B of RPO at September 30, 2026, about 40% is expected to be recognized over the next two years and another 40% over the following two. RPO includes deferred revenue and unbilled contract revenue, uses estimates for variable consideration, and excludes obligations with original terms of one year or less. Adding deal ceilings therefore gives a different figure. [4]

Three customers accounted for about 70% of Q3 revenue, with the largest at 33%. The filing does not identify them. Nebius also reported more than $12B of new commitments from the start of Q3 through the shareholder letter, and multiyear wins including Databricks, Datadog and Snowflake. [4][3]

The delivery measures that matter

Management expects 5 GW of contracted power by the end of 2026 and plans to bring more than 1 GW of new capacity online each year. Power must be energized, clusters installed and services accepted before the capacity becomes billable. The switch to permanent generation changed the New Jersey timeline. Management expects to deliver more than half of Microsoft’s capacity by year-end and the rest in Q2 2027. From 2027, most new capacity is expected to come from owned sites. [3]

Management estimates 19 months of payback for comparable new one-to-three-year deals signed in Q3, down from 22 months in Q2. The weighted annual contract value per MW for one-to-five-year deals rose from over $20M to over $25M. Actual utilization and costs after launch will determine realized returns. [3]

How financing risk reaches shareholders

Secured borrowing and refinancing
July’s roughly $773M loan priced at Term SOFR plus 2.50%. The borrower entities were in compliance with the minimum 1.15× debt service coverage covenant at September-end. The $3.5B Citi secured bridge signed in October has a 364-day maturity, a spread over Term SOFR starting at 2.75% and rising to 3.50%, and a minimum liquidity covenant. The disclosure sets out the facility size. Any draw would need repayment or refinancing in about a year. [4][7a][7b]
Convertibles still require repayment
The $5.75B August issue had initial conversion prices of $313.46 and $324.65. If held to maturity without conversion, about $6.67B of principal is due, plus coupons. A persistently lower share price makes conversion a less likely way to retire that debt. [4]
A lower price increases equity dilution
Nebius raised about $1.05B net in Q3 selling treasury shares at a weighted average of $229.14. For the same illustrative $1B gross raise, that price would require 4.36M shares, versus 11.18M at the November 27 close of $89.46, or 2.6 times as many. [4][9]

Nebius expensed $112.5M of interest in Q3 and capitalized another $91.9M of borrowing interest in construction assets. Both matter when assessing project returns. [4][15]

If 2027 revenue reaches the $12.5B guidance midpoint and year-end net debt rises to $12B on 335M economic shares, the $89.46 reference price requires about 3.36× EV/revenue. At 3.25×, the model gives roughly $85.45 a share.

Management’s timeline depends on the pace of delivery

Catalysts that could change the thesis

Signals that would weaken the case

Execution and concentration
Interconnection, liquid cooling or GPU supply delays could trigger customer discounts or move revenue into later periods. A handful of customers can change a quarter’s results materially.
Utilization and hardware life
New GPUs and custom chips can depress older cluster rental rates and residual values. Weaker renewals and longer payback on comparable new deals would undermine earnings quality.
Funding and per-share returns
Lower prepayment coverage, wider loan spreads, lower conversion prices or cheaper equity issuance can reduce the incremental value that goes to shareholders. [1][3][4][7a]

Research view. Nebius has shown that major customers will commit to buying compute in advance. Growth has a better chance of producing per-share returns if delayed capacity comes online on schedule and upfront payments and affordable funding hold up. The base-case value per share is about 4.5% below the updated closing price.
