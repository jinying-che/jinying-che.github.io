美股三大指数对比与 ETF 图解

标普 500 看美国大型股，纳斯达克综合指数看纳斯达克交易所的上市股票，道琼斯工业平均指数看 30 家美国蓝筹公司。三者选股范围和加权规则不同，涨跌也会不同。

指数与 ETF 为什么涨跌不同 券商怎么搜

三大指数，分别看什么？

同一只股票可能进入多个指数，权重却不同。按同一组维度比较，最容易看清每个指数代表的市场范围。点 ETF 代码可打开基金发行方页面。

  • 标普 500S&P 500 · SPX
    500 家美国大型公司，覆盖约 80% 的可投资美国股市市值。自由流通市值加权，可公开流通的市值越大，权重越高。
    VOO IVV SPY SPYM
  • 纳斯达克综合指数Nasdaq Composite · COMP
    按上市地点选股，覆盖纳斯达克交易所符合资格的普通股类证券。大大小小的公司都能入选，大型科技股对涨跌影响较大。市值加权，公司市值越大，影响通常越大。
    ONEQ
  • 道琼斯工业平均指数Dow Jones Industrial Average · DJIA
    由 30 家有代表性的美国蓝筹公司组成，覆盖多个行业，样本比前两者更窄。股价加权，每股价格越高，权重越大。
    DIA

指数简称与行情代码因平台而异。成分股数量会变化，同一公司也可能有多个股类入选。标普 500 的纳入规则还考虑盈利、流动性等条件。

同一天，为什么有的涨、有的跌？

先看指数有没有纳入那只股票，再看它的权重。市值加权看公司规模，自由流通市值只计可公开流通的股份。股价加权看每股价格，股价高的小公司也可能占较大权重。

在券商里，怎样找到对应的基金？

  1. 搜索 ETF 代码。 例如，标普 500 搜 VOO，纳斯达克综合搜 ONEQ，道琼斯工业平均搜 DIA。指数点位用于观察市场，ETF 以每份的市场价格成交。
  2. 核对基金全名和跟踪基准。 打开发行方页面，找到 Benchmark 或 Underlying Index，并和上表的指数全名核对。
  3. 同一基准下比较交易和持有成本。 看年费率、买卖价差、成交活跃度、交易佣金与换汇费用。基金成交价可能高于或低于每份净值，可交易性也取决于券商和账户所在地。

核对纳斯达克 ETF 的基准。 ONEQ 对应 Nasdaq Composite。QQQ 和 QQQM 跟踪的是 Nasdaq-100。

买 ETF 和买单只成分股有什么区别？

买一只指数 ETF，你持有的是基金份额，获得一篮子证券的组合敞口。买单只股票则承担这家公司的涨跌。例如，买 AAPL 获得的是苹果公司敞口，买跟踪标普 500 的 ETF 则获得该指数覆盖的组合敞口。

为什么 ETF 收益和新闻里的指数涨幅对不上？

先核对比较区间、币种和分红口径。价格指数只计价格变动，总回报指数假设分红再投资。ETF 还会受到费用、跟踪差异和交易价格相对净值的折溢价影响。做比较时，尽量使用同一币种、同一期间和相同分红口径。

Three Major U.S. Stock Indexes and Their ETFs

The S&P 500 tracks large U.S. companies. The Nasdaq Composite follows stocks listed on Nasdaq. The Dow Jones Industrial Average follows 30 U.S. blue-chip companies. Their selection and weighting rules can produce different returns on the same day.

What does each index track?

The same stock can appear in several indexes with different weights. Comparing the three on the same criteria shows which part of the market each represents. Select any ETF ticker to open its issuer’s page.

Index symbols vary by platform. The number of securities can change, and a company can have more than one share class in an index. S&P 500 eligibility also considers factors such as profitability and liquidity.

Why can they move in opposite directions?

Check which stocks are included, then look at their weights. Market-cap weighting reflects company size, and float adjustment counts only publicly tradable shares. Price weighting uses the price per share, so a smaller company with a higher share price can carry more weight.

How do you find the ETF at a broker?

  1. Search for the ETF ticker. For example, use VOO for the S&P 500, ONEQ for the Nasdaq Composite, and DIA for the Dow Jones Industrial Average. Index points show market performance. An ETF trades at a market price per share.
  2. Check the full fund name and benchmark. Look for “Benchmark” or “Underlying Index” on the issuer’s page, then match the full index name to the comparison above.
  3. Compare costs for funds with the same benchmark. Check the expense ratio, bid-ask spread, trading activity, commissions, and currency conversion fees. The ETF may trade above or below its net asset value per share. Availability depends on your broker and account jurisdiction.

Check the Nasdaq ETF’s benchmark. ONEQ follows the Nasdaq Composite. QQQ and QQQM track the Nasdaq-100.

How does buying an ETF differ from buying one stock?

An index ETF gives you a share of a fund with exposure to a basket of securities. An individual stock ties your results to one company. AAPL gives you exposure to Apple, while an S&P 500 ETF gives you exposure to the companies covered by that index.

Why might ETF returns differ from index returns in the news?

Check the period, currency, and treatment of dividends. A price index measures price changes alone. A total return index assumes reinvested dividends. ETF returns can also reflect fees, tracking differences, and premiums or discounts to net asset value. Use matching periods, currencies, and dividend assumptions when comparing them.
