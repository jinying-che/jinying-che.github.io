三大指数，分别看什么？

同一只股票可能进入多个指数。选股范围决定哪些股票能入选，加权规则决定每只股票占多大比重。

  • 指数覆盖范围与加权方式
  • 标普 500S&P 500 · SPX
    500 家美国大型公司，覆盖约 80% 的可投资美国股市市值。自由流通市值加权，可公开流通的市值越大，权重越高。
  • 纳斯达克综合指数Nasdaq Composite · COMP
    按上市地点选股，覆盖纳斯达克交易所符合资格的普通股类证券。大大小小的公司都能入选，大型科技股对涨跌影响较大。市值加权，公司市值越大，影响通常越大。
  • 道琼斯工业平均指数Dow Jones Industrial Average · DJIA
    由 30 家有代表性的美国蓝筹公司组成，覆盖多个行业，样本比前两者更窄。股价加权，每股价格越高，权重越大。

指数简称与行情代码因平台而异。成分股数量会变化，同一公司也可能有多个股类入选。标普 500 的纳入规则还考虑盈利、流动性等条件。

这些 ETF 有什么区别？

同一个标普 500 基准VOO · IVV · SPY · SPYM

四只基金的持仓和权重通常接近。按每持有 1 万美元估算年费用，SPYM 约 2 美元，VOO 和 IVV 各 3 美元，SPY 约 9.45 美元。SPY 交易更活跃，基金年费与买卖价差分别影响持有和交易成本。

两种纳斯达克敞口QQQ · QQQM · ONEQ

QQQ 和 QQQM 都跟踪纳斯达克 100，费率分别为 0.18% 和 0.15%，每 1 万美元年费用约差 3 美元。ONEQ 跟踪纳斯达克综合指数，覆盖范围扩展至较小公司和符合条件的金融股。

全市场与小盘股VTI · ITOT · IWM

VTI 和 ITOT 都覆盖美国不同规模的公司，费率均为 0.03%。两者的指数编制方不同，成分和权重会有差别。IWM 把敞口集中在美国小盘股，费率为 0.19%。

相同成分，权重不同RSP · VOO · IVV · SPY · SPYM

RSP 每季把标普 500 成分公司的权重调回接近相等。普通标普 500 基金按自由流通市值加权，因此更集中于规模最大的公司。即便成分公司相同，组合敞口也会不同。

30 家蓝筹与宽基DIA · VOO · VTI

DIA 只覆盖道指的 30 家公司，权重由每股价格决定。VOO 覆盖约 500 家大型公司，VTI 还纳入中、小和微盘股。基金之间可能持有相同的大公司，覆盖范围和权重仍有明显差别。

Three Major U.S. Stock Indexes and Their ETFs

The S&P 500 tracks large U.S. companies. The Nasdaq Composite follows stocks listed on Nasdaq. The Dow Jones Industrial Average follows 30 U.S. blue-chip companies.

Three indexes ETF profiles Compare ETFs Sector ETFs

What does each index track?

A stock can appear in several indexes. Selection rules determine which stocks qualify. Weighting rules determine each stock’s share of the index.

  • IndexCoverage and weighting
  • S&P 500SPX
    500 large U.S. companies, covering roughly 80% of the investable U.S. equity market by market value.Float-adjusted market-cap weighting gives more weight to companies with more publicly tradable market value.
  • Nasdaq CompositeCOMP
    Eligible common-stock-type securities listed on Nasdaq. Companies of many sizes can qualify, and large technology stocks have an outsized influence on returns.Market-cap weighting generally gives larger companies more influence.
  • Dow Jones Industrial AverageDJIA
    30 representative U.S. blue-chip companies from several industries, a narrower sample than the other two indexes.Price weighting gives more weight to stocks with higher prices per share.

Index symbols vary by platform. The number of securities can change, and a company can have more than one share class in an index. S&P 500 eligibility also considers factors such as profitability and liquidity.

How the ETFs compare

Same S&P 500 benchmarkVOO · IVV · SPY · SPYM

All four have broadly similar holdings and weights. Per $10,000 held, estimated annual fund costs are $2 for SPYM, $3 each for VOO and IVV, and $9.45 for SPY. SPY trades more actively. The expense ratio affects holding costs, while the bid-ask spread affects trading costs.

Two Nasdaq exposuresQQQ · QQQM · ONEQ

QQQ and QQQM both track the Nasdaq-100 and charge 0.18% and 0.15%, respectively, a difference of about $3 a year per $10,000. ONEQ tracks the Nasdaq Composite, which also covers smaller companies and eligible financial stocks.

Total market and small capsVTI · ITOT · IWM

VTI and ITOT cover U.S. companies across market-cap sizes and both charge 0.03%. Their index providers differ, so holdings and weights can vary. IWM concentrates on U.S. small caps and charges 0.19%.

Same companies, different weightsRSP · VOO · IVV · SPY · SPYM

RSP resets S&P 500 company weights to roughly equal levels each quarter. The standard S&P 500 funds give the largest companies more weight based on publicly tradable market value. The same companies can produce different portfolio exposures.

30 blue chips and broader fundsDIA · VOO · VTI

DIA covers 30 Dow companies and weights them by share price. VOO covers about 500 large companies, and VTI extends to mid-, small-, and micro-caps. The funds can hold some of the same large companies with very different coverage and weights.

Annual cost examples assume a constant $10,000 holding and multiply it by the expense ratio. Trading and account costs are separate.
