回撤到了哪里，盈利证据还缺什么？

陈浩的判断是，估值调整已到中段，后续深度可能小于 2022 年。这一判断仍需盈利预期支持。原稿未取得可核实的 EPS 一致预期序列。

历史刻度

选取的六轮历史回撤中位数约 −29.8%，其中四轮超过 25%，一轮超过 50%。本轮进一步回撤只是情景之一。

估值与盈利

原稿认为头部企业的现金流为本轮提供缓冲。头部个股的价格回撤可用于比较市场表现，盈利下修幅度仍需直接数据。

成分集中

原稿把纳斯达克 100 成分划为半导体链、云厂与其他。前两组合计约 55.5%，当日下跌集中于相关权重股。

情景判断

原稿把磨底列为概率最高的单一情景（35%），重点跟踪云厂资本开支、半导体库存与盈利预期。

Where is the drawdown, and what evidence is missing?

Chen Hao’s view is that the valuation reset is partway through and may be shallower than in 2022. The earnings case remains unverified because the deck did not obtain a reproducible consensus EPS series.

Historical scale

The six selected historical episodes had a median drawdown of about 29.8%. Four exceeded 25% and one exceeded 50%. Further losses remain one possible path.

Valuation and earnings

The deck sees cash flow at large companies as a buffer. Stock drawdowns describe market behavior. Earnings revisions still require a direct data series.

Concentration

In the deck’s Nasdaq-100 grouping, semiconductors and cloud providers account for about 55.5%. The day’s losses were concentrated in related heavyweights.

Scenario view

The deck puts prolonged bottoming first among its individual scenarios (35%), with cloud capital spending, semiconductor inventories, and earnings expectations as signals.
