Snowflake

企业用它整理数据、运行分析和部署 AI

Snowflake 托管数据和计算，运行在 AWS、Azure 和 Google Cloud 上。报表和批处理可以在独立集群上运行，共用同一份数据。客户按计算、存储和适用的数据传输用量付费。34

深入阅读 · 从合同和账单看按量商业模式

合同与消耗 大多数收入来自预先约定使用额度和价格的容量合同，通常按年预开账单，再随资源使用确认收入。按需客户则按月后付。FY27 Q2，按需安排约占总收入的 3%。24

客户怎样控费 报表集群和夜间批处理各自选规格、配置自动挂起。虚拟仓库运行时按秒计费，每次启动至少计 60 秒。若查询频繁，反复挂起可能反而增加开销；无服务器和 AI 功能有各自用量计价。2324

深入阅读 · 四类产品怎样接成一条数据链

从文件到分析表 Snowpipe 把新到文件持续装入表，Dynamic Tables 按声明的查询与目标延迟更新下游分析表。目标越紧，刷新可能越频繁。522

从共用表到不同任务 报表用 SQL，复杂转换可用 Snowpark 的 Python、Java 或 Scala API。两者可以使用隔离的计算资源，继续访问同一份受治理数据。36

从权限到业务定义 行级访问和字段脱敏约束数据能给谁看，语义视图定义“净销量”等指标。Cortex Analyst 用这些定义生成 SQL；Cortex Search 让智能体检索商品说明、补货手册等文本。7935

为什么企业 AI 能带动 Snowflake 增长

Snowflake 的机会来自存量客户。企业已经在平台上整理数据、定义业务指标和配置权限，就可以在同一环境为 AI 增加新任务，减少为每个应用重复搬运数据和重做治理的工作。379

  1. 沿用已有的数据和权限

    报表已使用的库存和订单表可供 AI 查询；通过语义定义，智能体可以沿用“缺货”等业务口径。79

  2. 把定期报表变成日常问答

    店长问“哪些门店将缺货”，Cortex Analyst 把问题转成 SQL，Cortex Search 可检索补货手册。9

  3. 更多任务带来更多用量

    反复运行的任务可带来 SQL 查询、文档检索和模型推理用量，具体按实际调用的服务计费。410

成长的条件　试用需要变成持续付费用量，新增收入要覆盖推理和云成本。推理效率提升也可能降低单次任务用量。账户可能重叠，无法用这些数字推算付费客户数或 AI 收入。现有财报未单列 AI 收入。1210

已披露案例 · Thomson Reuters 客户支持团队用 Snowflake 的语言模型函数预处理工单，再把 Cortex Analyst 接入自建智能体，帮助团队识别常见客户问题。该公司案例展示了工作流，未披露 Snowflake 获得的收入。25

深入阅读 · AI 收入的三条路径与证据边界

拉动现有用量 智能体提出问题时，前置表仍需更新，SQL 仍需计算。即便前台由客户自建，底层任务也可能继续在 Snowflake 上运行。91025

直接销售 AI 服务 模型推理可以按处理的 token 计费，搜索还涉及索引和服务费用。管理层称 FY27 Q2 核心业务与 AI 均带动增速提升，尚未给出独立 AI 收入金额。1910

扩展使用人群 CoCo 面向开发，CoWork 面向业务团队。今年 11 月发布会上展示的 Bots、Cortex Sense 包含拟开放预览的能力。检验成长要看真实生产频率、每项任务的毛利和结果准确性。11126

ENTERPRISE CLOUD DATA PLATFORM

Snowflake

Where enterprises prepare data, run analytics, and deploy AI

Snowflake manages data and compute on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Reporting and batch jobs can run on separate clusters against the same data. Customers pay for compute, storage, and applicable data transfer.34

Read more · How contracts and usage billing work

Contracts and consumption Most revenue comes from capacity contracts with agreed usage and prices. Snowflake typically bills these annually in advance and recognizes revenue as customers consume resources. On-demand arrangements are billed monthly in arrears and accounted for about 3% of Q2 revenue.24

How customers control spend Reporting and overnight batch jobs can size and suspend their clusters independently. Warehouses are billed by the second while running with a 60-second minimum per start. Frequent suspends can backfire for steady query traffic. Serverless and AI features have separate usage meters.2324

Read more · How the four product areas work together

From files to business tables Snowpipe continuously loads arriving files. Dynamic Tables maintain downstream tables using a declared query and freshness target. Tighter targets can require more frequent refreshes.522

One dataset, different workloads Reporting uses SQL, while complex transformations can use Snowpark’s Python, Java, or Scala APIs. Workloads can use isolated compute and query the same governed data.36

From permissions to business definitions Row-level policies and column masking govern who can see data. Semantic views define measures such as net units sold. Cortex Analyst uses those definitions to generate SQL, and Cortex Search retrieves text such as product descriptions and replenishment guides.7935

Why enterprise AI can drive growth for Snowflake

Snowflake’s opportunity starts with existing customers. Companies that already organize data, define metrics, and configure access on Snowflake can add AI tasks in the same environment, reducing the need to copy data and rebuild governance for each application.379

  1. Reuse data and access rules

    Agents can query the inventory and orders already used in reports. Semantic definitions give terms such as “out of stock” consistent meaning.79

  2. Turn periodic reports into everyday Q&A

    A manager asks which stores may run out. Cortex Analyst translates the question into SQL, and Cortex Search can retrieve the replenishment manual.9

  3. More tasks can drive more usage

    Recurring tasks can consume SQL compute, document retrieval, and model inference. Billing follows the services invoked.410

What has to go right · Trials need to become recurring paid usage, with revenue covering added inference and cloud costs. More efficient inference may lower consumption per task. Accounts may overlap and cannot establish paid customer counts or AI revenue. Current filings do not break out AI revenue.1210

Disclosed example · Thomson Reuters Its support team uses Snowflake’s language-model functions to prepare support cases, then connects Cortex Analyst to a custom agent to surface common customer issues. The case study describes the workflow and provides no revenue figure for Snowflake.25

Read more · Three paths to AI revenue and what the evidence shows

More use of the core platform An agent’s question can still require fresh upstream data and SQL compute. Even customers that build their own front ends can keep running the underlying work on Snowflake.91025

Direct use of AI services Inference can be metered by tokens, while search can add indexing and serving costs. Management attributed Q2 acceleration to both its core business and AI, without giving a standalone AI revenue figure.1910

Reach more users CoCo targets developers and CoWork targets business teams. November’s announced capabilities included Bots and Cortex Sense with previews planned. Production frequency, gross profit per task and answer quality are the tests.11126

The moat is in everyday workflows

Why customers stay

The more reports, pipelines, policies, and sharing relationships a customer runs, the more workflows it must rebuild and validate when moving. Secure sharing lets partners query selected data and reduces the need to maintain copies.28

Open formats such as Iceberg let customers use other engines for some data. Each new workload is still a choice based on cost, performance, and existing tools.212

How to read net revenue retention A 126% reading implies about 26% net expansion in product revenue for the same cohort of capacity-contract customers versus the previous 12 months. Expansion, contraction and churn all affect it.2
