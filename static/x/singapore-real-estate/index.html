6.45% 的分派率意味着什么What a 6.45% cash yield means

用参考价 S$0.667 算，现金分派率高出 10 年期国债约 4.30 个百分点。若年分派降到 S$0.039，这个利差就缩到约 3.70 个百分点，接近 Phillip 估计的历史平均 3.79 个百分点。

At the S$0.667 reference price, the trailing cash yield sits about 4.30 percentage points above the 10-year government bond. At S$0.039 of annual distributions, the spread shrinks to about 3.70 points, close to Phillip’s 3.79-point historical average.

物业估值会放大杠杆的影响。 假设净租金不变，资本化率从 4.0% 升到 4.5%，物业价值约跌 11%。若初始债务占资产 40% 且债务保持原额，账面权益约跌 19%。

Leverage amplifies property revaluations. With flat net rents, moving the capitalisation rate from 4.0% to 4.5% cuts property value by about 11%. With initial debt at 40% of assets and the debt amount unchanged, book equity falls by about 19%.

减仓 · 资金需求或分派走弱Trim · Cash needs or weaker payouts

未来一两年要用钱、地产敞口已集中，或分派预期降到 S$0.038–0.039 且融资与利息覆盖同时走弱。以参考价计，这样的分派约对应 5.7%–5.8% 的收益率。

Consider trimming if you need the cash within a year or two, already have concentrated property exposure, or expect payouts of S$0.038–0.039 alongside worsening funding and coverage. That payout would yield roughly 5.7%–5.8% at the reference price.
